  ### 3. When You Are Ready to Publish (Build Google Play AAB Bundle)

  Once you finish testing and are ready to upload to Google Play Console:

    cd D:\Bideo\bideoApp\android
    .\gradlew bundleRelease

  │ Where to find the Google Play .aab file:
  │ D:\Bideo\bideoApp\android\app\build\outputs\bundle\release\app-release.aab

  Upload this .aab file to Google Play Console → Production.
