Bideo App packs a lot into one place. This guide walks through every major feature so you know exactly what you can do — as a viewer and as a creator.

## The main tabs

At the bottom of the app you'll find five tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | A feed of videos and shorts picked for you |
| **Shorts** | A full-screen, swipeable feed of short videos |
| **Upload (+)** | Upload a short, a long video or a community post |
| **Followings** | New content from creators you follow |
| **Library** | Your channel, playlists, history and liked videos |

## Shorts

Shorts are **vertical (9:16) videos of up to 60 seconds**. Swipe up for the next one. Shorts are the fastest way for new creators to be discovered — see [how to go viral on Bideo](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-bideo).

## Long videos

Upload standard landscape or portrait videos (up to 1080p, MP4 or MOV) for tutorials, vlogs, reviews, music and more. Add a clear title, tags and a thumbnail so people find them.

## Stories

Share a photo or video **story that disappears after 24 hours**. Your followers see it at the top of their feed. Learn more in the [stories and community posts guide](/blog/bideo-stories-and-community-posts).

## Community posts

Post a text or image update to your followers — announcements, polls in text, behind-the-scenes photos. You can share **one community post per day**.

## Chat and groups

Send messages to other users, share videos directly in chat, and create **group chats**. New conversations start as message requests that the other person can accept. See the [chat guide](/blog/bideo-chat-messaging-guide).

## Playlists, history and liked videos

Save videos into **playlists**, see what you watched in **History**, and find everything you liked in **Liked videos** — all in the Library tab. Details: [playlists and history guide](/blog/bideo-playlists-history-liked-videos).

## Coins, boosts and the verified badge

- **Coins** — earn them by watching optional rewarded ads and through watch rewards. [How to earn coins](/blog/how-to-earn-coins-on-bideo).
- **Boost** — spend coins to give a video extra visibility for 1 to 24 hours. [Boost guide](/blog/bideo-video-boost-guide).
- **Verified badge** — a blue check for your channel. [Verified badge guide](/blog/bideo-verified-badge-guide).

## Weekly creator leaderboard

Bideo ranks creators by their views over the last 7 days, and also shows the most-followed creators. Top creators are highlighted on their channel. [How the leaderboard works](/blog/bideo-weekly-creator-leaderboard).

## Creator earnings

Eligible adult creators can apply for monetization and earn from views on approved videos, then withdraw to UPI or a bank account. Start with [how to earn money on Bideo](/blog/how-to-earn-money-on-bideo).

## Safety tools

Report any video from its options menu, block users in chat, and read the community guidelines. See [is Bideo App safe?](/blog/is-bideo-app-safe)
