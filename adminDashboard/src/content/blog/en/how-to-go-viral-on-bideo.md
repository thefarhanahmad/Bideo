There's no button that makes a video go viral — but there are habits that make it far more likely. These tips are based on what keeps viewers watching, sharing and following on short-video platforms like Bideo.

## 1. Win the first 2 seconds

Most viewers decide whether to keep watching almost instantly. Start with the most interesting moment: a question, a surprising visual, a funny reaction, or the final result first ("this is how it ended up — here's how I made it").

## 2. Keep it tight

Shorts on Bideo can be up to **60 seconds**, but shorter is often better. Cut every pause and filler word. If a moment doesn't add to the story, remove it.

## 3. Give viewers a reason to watch till the end

Tease a payoff early ("wait for the last one") and deliver it. Videos that are watched to the end — or watched twice — tend to be shown to more people.

## 4. Ride trends, add your twist

Use trending formats, sounds and topics, but add something only you can do: your language, your city, your humour. A trend plus a unique angle beats a plain copy.

## 5. Make it relatable

Everyday Indian moments — family, school, office, festivals, food, cricket — are shareable because people see themselves in them.

## 6. Use clear titles and relevant hashtags

Write a title that says what the video is about, and add 3–5 relevant hashtags. See the [titles and hashtags guide](/blog/short-video-titles-and-hashtags-guide).

## 7. Post when your audience is online

Evenings and weekends are often busy, but every audience is different. Read [the best time to post short videos in India](/blog/best-time-to-post-short-videos-in-india) and test.

## 8. Post consistently

Creators who post regularly — for example 3 to 5 shorts a week — get more chances to hit. One video rarely makes a channel; a steady stream does.

## 9. Start conversations in the comments

Ask a simple question at the end ("which one would you pick?"). Reply to early comments and pin the best one — active comment sections keep people around.

## 10. Use stories and posts to bring fans back

Share a behind-the-scenes [story](/blog/bideo-stories-and-community-posts) or a community post when you publish. Your existing followers are your first viewers.

## 11. Give a good video a boost

If a video is already performing well, a [Boost](/blog/bideo-video-boost-guide) (paid with coins) can show it to more people for 1 to 24 hours.

## 12. Learn from your own numbers

Look at which videos got the most views, likes and comments, and make more in that style. Repeat what works; drop what doesn't.

> **Important:** Never use fake views, fake followers or copied content. These break the community guidelines and can get videos removed or accounts suspended — the opposite of going viral.
