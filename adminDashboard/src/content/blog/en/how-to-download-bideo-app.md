Downloading **Bideo App** takes less than a minute. This guide shows the safest way to install the official app on your Android phone, and how to fix the most common problems.

## Before you start

- An **Android phone** running Android 7.0 or newer.
- A working internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
- Some free storage space on your phone.

## Download Bideo from Google Play

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your phone.
2. Tap the search bar and type **Bideo**.
3. Find the official app, published under the name **“Bideo – India's Video Platform”**.
4. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to finish.
5. Tap **Open** to launch the app.

You can also open **bideo.in** on your phone and tap **Download App** — it takes you directly to the official Play Store page.

> **Safety tip:** Always install Bideo from the Google Play Store. Avoid APK files from unknown websites — they can be outdated, modified or unsafe.

## After installing: first steps

1. **Choose how to sign in** — with your mobile number or your Google account. See the [account setup guide](/blog/how-to-create-bideo-account).
2. **Allow notifications** if you want alerts for new videos, comments and messages. You can change this later in your phone settings.
3. **Start watching** from the **Home** and **Shorts** tabs.

## Fix common installation problems

**“Install” button not working or download stuck**
- Check your internet connection and try again.
- Clear the Play Store cache: *Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache*.

**“Not enough storage”**
- Delete unused apps, old videos or downloads, then try again.

**“Your device isn't compatible”**
- Bideo needs Android 7.0 or newer. Check your version in *Settings → About phone*.

**App opens but videos don't play**
- Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and make sure the app is updated to the latest version.

## Keep Bideo updated

Updates bring new features and fixes. In Play Store, open **Manage apps & device** and update Bideo when an update is available, or turn on auto-update.
