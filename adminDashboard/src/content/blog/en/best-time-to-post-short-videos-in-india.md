Posting at the right time helps a new video get its first viewers quickly — and early views and comments help it reach more people. Here's how to choose times that suit Indian audiences, and how to find the best time for **your** viewers.

## Good starting times (India, IST)

These are common times when many people in India have a few free minutes on their phones. Treat them as a starting point, not a rule:

| Time slot (IST) | Why it can work |
|---|---|
| **7:00 – 9:00 AM** | Morning routine, commute and chai breaks |
| **12:30 – 2:00 PM** | Lunch breaks at college and work |
| **6:00 – 10:00 PM** | Evening free time — often the busiest window |
| **Weekends, late morning** | Relaxed scrolling time |

## Match the time to your audience

- **Students:** after school/college hours and late evenings.
- **Office workers:** lunch time and after 7 PM.
- **Homemakers and recipe viewers:** late morning and early afternoon.
- **Devotional content:** early mornings and festival days.

## Find your own best time in 3 steps

1. **Pick two or three time slots** from the table above.
2. **Post at each slot for one to two weeks**, keeping your content quality similar.
3. **Compare the first-day views and comments** for each slot and keep the winner.

> **Tip:** Post 15–30 minutes before your audience's busy time, so your video is ready when they open the app.

## Timing tips that help any video

- Reply to comments in the first hour after posting.
- Share a [story or community post](/blog/bideo-stories-and-community-posts) right after uploading.
- Plan posts around festivals, cricket matches and trending events in your niche.
- Keep a steady schedule so followers know when to expect you.
