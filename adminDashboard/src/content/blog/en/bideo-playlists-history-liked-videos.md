Found a video you love? Bideo makes it easy to save it and find it again later. Everything lives in the **Library** tab.

## Playlists

- **Create a playlist** for any theme — recipes, study, workouts, comedy.
- **Add videos** to a playlist while watching.
- Make a playlist **private** if it's just for you.

### Playlist ideas
- "Watch later" for videos you don't have time for now
- "Recipes to try" for your cooking inspiration
- "Exam prep" for study videos
- "Favourites" for videos you rewatch

## Watch history

Your **watch history** shows videos you recently watched, so you can quickly go back to something you saw earlier — even if you forgot its name.

## Liked videos

Every video you like is saved in **Liked videos**. It's a quick way to revisit content you enjoyed and discover more from those creators.

## Tips

- Like videos you enjoy — it also helps you find them later.
- Follow creators whose videos you keep saving; their new uploads appear in **Followings**.
- Use playlists to plan what to watch with family or friends.

## Smart ways to use your Library

- **Plan family viewing:** make a playlist of recipes, devotional songs or kids' learning videos to watch together later.
- **Study smarter:** keep one playlist per subject so revision videos are always in one place.
- **Discover new creators:** check your history and liked videos for creators you enjoy, then follow them so their new uploads appear in **Followings**.
- **Save before you forget:** if you're busy, add a video to a "Watch later" playlist instead of losing it in the feed.

## For creators: make your videos easy to save

Viewers save videos that are **useful to come back to** — recipes, tutorials, tips and series. Clear titles help people recognise your video later in their playlists and history. See the [titles and hashtags guide](/blog/short-video-titles-and-hashtags-guide).
