A Bideo account lets you like, comment, follow creators, chat and upload your own videos. Setting it up properly from day one also helps people find and remember your channel.

## Two ways to sign up

### 1. Sign up with your mobile number

1. Open Bideo and tap **Sign up**.
2. Enter a **username**, your **10-digit mobile number** and a **password**.
3. Tap to create your account.

### 2. Continue with Google

1. Open Bideo and choose **Continue with Google**.
2. Pick the Google account you want to use.

Both methods give you the same features. Use whichever is easier for you to remember.

## Choosing a good username

Your username identifies you on Bideo. The rules are simple:

- **3 to 30 characters**
- Letters, numbers, **underscores (_)** and **periods (.)**
- **No spaces**

Tips for a username people remember:
- Keep it short and easy to spell (for example `chef.priya` or `comedy_ravi`).
- Match the name you use on other platforms, if possible.
- Avoid random numbers unless your name is already taken.

## Set up your channel

Once you are signed in, open your channel and edit your profile:

- **Channel name** — the name viewers see. Make it clear what your channel is about.
- **Profile photo** — a clear face photo or simple logo works best.
- **Cover image** — a wide banner that shows your style or topic.
- **Bio (About)** — one or two lines about what you post and how often.
- **Social links** — add your other profiles so fans can follow you everywhere.

> **Tip:** A complete profile builds trust. Viewers are more likely to follow a channel with a photo, a clear name and a short bio.

## Keep your account secure

- Use a password you don't use anywhere else.
- Never share your password or verification codes with anyone — Bideo staff will never ask for them.
- If you forget your password, tap **Forgot password** on the login screen.
