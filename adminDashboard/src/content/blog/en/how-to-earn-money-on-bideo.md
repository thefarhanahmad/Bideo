Bideo has a **creator monetization program** that lets eligible creators earn from the views on their approved videos and withdraw to their **UPI ID or bank account**. This guide explains the full process honestly — what you need, how it works and what to expect.

> **Please note:** Bideo does not promise guaranteed or instant income. What you earn depends on eligibility, views on your monetized videos and the current reward rates, which may change.

## Who can earn on Bideo

To take part in monetization you need to:

- Be an **adult** (minors cannot access monetized features).
- Upload **original content** you own or have permission to use.
- Follow the **community guidelines** and copyright rules.
- Complete the **verification and payout details** in the app.

## The 2 steps to get monetized

Open **Creator Earnings & Payouts** in the app to see your progress.

### Step 1: Upload 3 original videos that pass review

Each video goes through a quick **monetization review**. In the app, you can complete a video's review by watching **4 short rewarded ads** (“Pass via Ads”), or the video can be approved by the review team. You need **3 approved original videos** to complete Step 1.

Full details: [Bideo monetization requirements](/blog/bideo-monetization-requirements).

### Step 2: Submit your verification and payout details

Once Step 1 is done, the payout section unlocks. Fill in:

- Your **full legal name** (as per bank/Aadhaar)
- Your **registered phone number**
- Your **Aadhaar number** for verification
- Your **UPI ID** or **bank account details**

Tap **Submit Verification Details**. The team reviews your application, and once approved your channel shows as **Monetized**.

## How earnings reach your wallet

After monetization, views on your eligible videos and shorts generate earnings:

1. Earnings are first recorded as **pending**.
2. Pending earnings are **settled into your wallet daily** (after midnight IST).
3. Your **Available Wallet Balance** shows what you can withdraw.

## How to withdraw your money

When your available balance reaches **₹1,000**:

1. Open **Creator Earnings & Payouts** and tap **Request Payout**.
2. Choose **UPI** or **bank transfer** as the payout destination.
3. Enter the amount and tap **Submit Withdrawal Request**.
4. Track the status in **Payout History**.

Step-by-step: [how to withdraw money from Bideo](/blog/how-to-withdraw-money-from-bideo).

## Tips to earn more (the honest way)

- Post **consistently** — more quality videos means more chances for views.
- Focus on **watch time**: strong hooks and tight editing. See [how to go viral](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-bideo).
- **Grow followers** who come back for every upload — [follower guide](/blog/how-to-grow-followers-on-bideo).
- Use a [Boost](/blog/bideo-video-boost-guide) on videos that are already performing well.

## What can stop your earnings

- Re-uploading TV shows, movie clips or other creators' content
- Fake views, fake followers or spam
- Breaking the community guidelines

These can lead to **demonetization** and possible **account suspension**.

## Coins vs earnings

**Coins** are an in-app currency used for features like Boost and the verified badge. They are separate from your **rupee wallet earnings**. Learn more in [how to earn coins on Bideo](/blog/how-to-earn-coins-on-bideo).
