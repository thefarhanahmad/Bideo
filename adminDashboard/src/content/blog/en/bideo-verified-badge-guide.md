The **blue verified badge** shows viewers that a channel is established on Bideo. It appears next to your name on your profile, channel, videos, shorts and comments. There are two ways to get it.

## Option 1: Unlock it with coins

- Cost: **1,500 coins**
- Duration: **30 days**
- Unlocking it again while it's active **extends it by another 30 days**

Earn coins for free through rewarded ads and watch rewards — see [how to earn coins on Bideo](/blog/how-to-earn-coins-on-bideo).

## Option 2: Earn it with views

Channels can receive the verified badge **automatically when they reach 100,000 total views**. Keep posting great content — [how to go viral](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-bideo) and [how to grow followers](/blog/how-to-grow-followers-on-bideo) can help you get there.

## How to unlock the badge with coins

1. Check your **coin balance** in the app (you need 1,500 coins).
2. Open the **verified badge** option where boosts and coins are shown.
3. Confirm the unlock — the blue check appears on your channel for **30 days**.
4. Before it ends, you can unlock it again to **add another 30 days**.

## Which option should you choose?

| If you… | Best option |
|---|---|
| Are new and want to look established quickly | Unlock with 1,500 coins |
| Already get steady views | Keep growing — the badge comes automatically at 100,000 total views |
| Want long-term trust | Combine the badge with consistent, original uploads |

## What the badge does (and doesn't do)

**Does:**
- Adds a blue check next to your name across the app
- Helps your channel look established and trustworthy

**Doesn't:**
- Guarantee more views or earnings
- Replace the [monetization requirements](/blog/bideo-monetization-requirements)

## Keep your badge

The badge can be removed if a channel breaks the community guidelines, uses fake engagement or impersonates someone else.

> **Beware:** Nobody can sell you a Bideo blue tick outside the app. Only unlock it inside the official Bideo app.

## Next steps

Pair the badge with a well-timed [Boost](/blog/bideo-video-boost-guide) to make a strong first impression on new viewers.

## Make the most of your badge

- **Complete your profile** — photo, cover, bio and links — so the badge sits on a channel that looks professional.
- **Post a strong introduction video** that tells new viewers what your channel is about.
- **Stay consistent** — the badge earns trust, but regular quality videos keep viewers coming back.
