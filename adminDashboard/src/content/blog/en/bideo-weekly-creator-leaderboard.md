Bideo celebrates creators who are doing well right now with a **weekly leaderboard**. Here's how it works and how you can climb it.

## Two lists

### 1. Weekly top creators (by views)

- Ranks creators by **views on their public videos in the last 7 days**.
- If two creators have the same views, the one with **more followers** ranks higher.
- **Top creators are highlighted** on their channel.

### 2. Top creators by followers

- Shows the **most-followed creators** on Bideo.
- Also shows how many **new followers they gained this week**.

## Why it matters

- It's a great way for viewers to **discover popular creators**.
- A top position brings **recognition** and helps new viewers trust your channel.

## How to climb the weekly rankings

1. **Post consistently this week** — the window is the last 7 days, so fresh uploads matter.
2. **Make public videos** — only public videos count.
3. **Hook viewers fast** and keep videos tight — see [how to go viral](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-bideo).
4. **Share new uploads** with a story and a community post.
5. **Boost a strong video** at the right time — see the [Boost guide](/blog/bideo-video-boost-guide).
6. **Grow followers** — they're your first viewers and also break ties.

> **Play fair:** Fake views and fake followers break the community guidelines and can lead to removal from rankings or suspension.
