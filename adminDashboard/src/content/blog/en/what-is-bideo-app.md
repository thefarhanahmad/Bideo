**Bideo App** is a free Indian video platform where you can watch and create **short videos (Shorts)**, **long videos**, **stories** and **community posts** — all from your Android phone. It is built for Indian creators and audiences, with content in many regional languages.

If you have just heard about Bideo, this guide explains what the app is, what you can do with it, and how to get started in a few minutes.

## What Bideo App is

Bideo (pronounced like "video" with a B) is a mobile-first video sharing platform. Viewers use it to discover entertaining and useful content, and creators use it to publish videos and build a following.

Bideo focuses on **original regional creativity** — comedy, dance, shayari, food, education, devotional content, vlogs and more — so that creators from small towns and big cities both get a fair chance to be discovered.

## What you can do on Bideo

- **Watch Shorts** — vertical videos (9:16) of up to 60 seconds, in a fast, swipeable feed.
- **Watch long videos** — standard landscape or portrait videos for tutorials, vlogs, stories and more.
- **Follow creators** — see new uploads from the channels you follow in the **Followings** tab.
- **Post stories** — photo or video stories that disappear after 24 hours.
- **Share community posts** — a text or image update to your followers (one post per day).
- **Chat** — send messages, share videos with friends and create group chats.
- **Save videos** — build playlists, revisit your watch history and find videos you liked.
- **Earn coins and grow** — collect coins, boost your videos and unlock the verified badge.

## Who Bideo is for

**Viewers** who want a light, fast app full of Indian content in their own language.

**Creators** who want to publish shorts and videos, grow followers and, once eligible, apply for the Bideo monetization program. Read [how to start a video channel as a beginner](/blog/how-to-start-a-video-channel-beginners) if you are new to creating.

## How Bideo works

1. **Download** the app from Google Play ([step-by-step guide](/blog/how-to-download-bideo-app)).
2. **Sign up** with your mobile number or Google account ([account setup guide](/blog/how-to-create-bideo-account)).
3. **Watch** from the Home and Shorts tabs, or **upload** with the + button.
4. **Grow** by posting consistently, using clear titles and engaging with your audience.

> **Tip:** You can browse and watch without uploading anything. An account is needed when you want to like, comment, follow, chat or upload.
