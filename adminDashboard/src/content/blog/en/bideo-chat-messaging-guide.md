Bideo has built-in chat so you can talk to friends, fans and fellow creators — and share videos without leaving the app.

## Start a conversation

Open **Chat** and start a new conversation with another user. When you message someone new, your chat starts as a **message request**.

## Message requests

- If someone new messages **you**, you'll see it as a request.
- **Accept** to keep chatting, or **decline** if you don't want to talk.
- This keeps your inbox under your control.

## Group chats

- Create a **group** with a name, photo and description.
- **Add members** and choose **group admins** who can manage the group.
- Great for creator collaborations, fan clubs or friends.

## Share videos in chat

Share any Bideo video or short directly into a chat — your friend can open and watch it in one tap.

## Manage your messages

- **Unsend** a message you sent.
- **Delete for me** to remove a message only from your view.
- Unread messages show as a badge so you never miss a reply.

## Stay safe in chat

- **Block** anyone who makes you uncomfortable — they won't be able to keep messaging you.
- **Report** their videos or posts if they break the rules, or contact Bideo through the [Contact page](/contact).
- Never share passwords, OTPs or bank details in chat — Bideo staff will never ask for them.
- Be careful with strangers offering money, followers or "verification".
