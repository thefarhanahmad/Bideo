**Coins** are Bideo's in-app currency. You can earn them for free and spend them to promote your videos or unlock the verified badge. Here's everything you need to know.

## Ways to earn coins

### 1. Watch rewarded ads

In the app you can choose to watch short **rewarded ads**:

- Each ad gives you **1 to 6 coins**.
- After **3 ads**, there's a **10-minute break**.
- After **16 ads**, the cycle **resets after 1 hour**.

Ads are always optional — watch them only if you want coins.

### 2. Watch rewards

Signed-in viewers can receive small **coin rewards for watching shorts**. Keep watching content you enjoy and coins add up over time.

## What you can do with coins

| Use | Coins |
|---|---|
| Boost a video for 1 hour | 100 |
| Boost for 3 hours | 250 |
| Boost for 6 hours | 500 |
| Boost for 24 hours | 1,000 |
| Verified badge (30 days) | 1,500 |

- **Boost** gives your video extra visibility for a limited time — see the [Boost guide](/blog/bideo-video-boost-guide).
- **Verified badge** adds a blue check to your channel — see the [verified badge guide](/blog/bideo-verified-badge-guide).

## Coins vs money

Coins are **separate from your rupee wallet**. Rupee earnings come from the [creator monetization program](/blog/how-to-earn-money-on-bideo), not from coins.

## Check your coin balance

Your coin balance and transaction history (coins earned and spent) are shown in the app, so you always know where your coins came from and where they went.

> **Beware of scams:** No website, app or person can give you "unlimited Bideo coins". These are fake and can steal your account. Earn coins only inside the official Bideo app.
