If you see something on Bideo that breaks the rules, reporting it helps keep the community safe. Here's how to report and what happens next.

## Report a video or short

1. Tap the **options menu (⋮)** on the video or short.
2. Choose **Report Content**.
3. Choose the reason that fits best — for anything involving minors, choose **Child Safety Concern**.
4. Submit the report.

## Problems with a user in chat

- Open the chat and **block** the user to stop further messages.
- If their videos or posts break the rules, report that content from its options menu.
- For anything else, contact Bideo through the [Contact page](/contact).

## What happens after you report

- Bideo's moderation team reviews the report against the [community guidelines](/guidelines).
- Depending on the issue, content may be removed or restricted, and accounts may be warned, suspended or terminated.
- **Child safety reports** are given the **highest priority** for immediate review and removal.

## Report responsibly

- Report only **real** violations and give **accurate details**.
- Don't use reports to harass creators you simply dislike — misuse of reporting is itself against the rules.

## Copyright complaints

If someone uploaded **your** copyrighted content, send a takedown notice using the details on the [Contact page](/contact), including the Bideo link to the content.

## Grievances

For other complaints, use the grievance details on the [Contact page](/contact). Grievances are resolved **within 15 days** of receipt.
