Videos bring new viewers; **stories** and **community posts** keep your existing followers close. Here's how both work on Bideo and how to use them well.

## Stories: quick, 24-hour updates

- Share a **photo or short video**, with an optional caption.
- Stories are visible for **24 hours**, then disappear automatically.
- You can see **who viewed** your story.

### Story ideas

- Behind the scenes of your next video
- "New video is live!" with a teaser
- A quick question or opinion to start conversations
- Your day, your city, your food
- Festival greetings for your followers

## Community posts: updates for your followers

- Post **text** with an optional **image**.
- Followers can **like** and **comment**.
- You can share **one community post per day**, so make it count.

### Community post ideas

- Announce your next series or upload schedule
- Ask followers what video they want next
- Share a useful tip in your niche
- Thank followers when you reach a milestone
- Post a photo from your latest shoot

## How stories and posts help you grow

1. **Remind followers** that you've uploaded something new.
2. **Start conversations** — comments build a loyal community.
3. **Stay visible** between video uploads.
4. **Learn what your audience wants** by asking directly.

> **Tip:** When you publish a new video, share a story the same day — your followers are your first viewers. More ideas in [how to go viral](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-bideo).

## Keep it respectful

Stories and posts follow the same community guidelines as videos. Don't share private information about others, misleading claims or harmful content.
