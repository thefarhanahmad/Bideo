If you no longer want to use Bideo, you can delete your account directly from the app. Here's exactly how, and what happens next.

## Before you delete

- Deletion is **permanent** after the grace period — your videos, posts and profile data will be removed.
- If you're a monetized creator, **withdraw your available balance** first (see [how to withdraw money](/blog/how-to-withdraw-money-from-bideo)).
- If you just want a break, you can simply log out instead.

## How to delete your account

1. Open the **Bideo** app and log in.
2. Go to the **Library** tab.
3. Tap the **Settings** icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner.
4. Under **Account Actions**, select **Delete Profile**.
5. Enter a **reason** and your **password**, tap **Delete Profile**, and confirm.

## The 5-day grace period

- Your account enters a **5-day grace period** (120 hours).
- Your profile is **locked and hidden** from public view straight away.
- **Changed your mind?** Submit an **Account Recovery** request during these 5 days.
- After 5 days, your data is **permanently deleted** and cannot be restored.

## Can't access the app?

Visit the [Account Deletion page](/account-deletion) or contact Bideo through the [Contact page](/contact) for help.

## Next steps

If something specific is bothering you — spam, harassment or unwanted messages — you may not need to delete your account. You can [report content](/blog/how-to-report-content-on-bideo) and block users instead.
