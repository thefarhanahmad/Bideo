It's smart to ask whether an app is safe before you use it. Here's how Bideo protects users, and simple steps you can take to stay safe.

## How Bideo keeps the platform safe

- **Community guidelines** that ban nudity, hate, harassment, dangerous acts, scams and copyright abuse.
- **In-app reporting** on every video and short.
- **Human moderation** that reviews reported videos, posts and channel profiles.
- **Strict child safety policy** with zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material and exploitation; child safety reports get the **highest priority**.
- **Blocking and message requests** in chat, so you control who can talk to you.
- **Monetization only for adults** — minors cannot access monetized features.

## Your privacy

Bideo uses your information to run the service — your account, uploads, feed and safety checks — as described in its [Privacy Policy](/privacy). You choose what you post publicly, and you can [delete your account](/blog/how-to-delete-bideo-account) at any time.

## Safety tips for everyone

1. **Never share your password or OTP** — Bideo staff will never ask for them.
2. **Ignore "free coins", "blue tick" or "earning" offers** from strangers — they're scams.
3. **Install only from Google Play** — not from APK websites.
4. **Keep personal details private** — address, school, phone number.
5. **Block** anyone who harasses you and **report** content that breaks the rules.

## Tips for parents

- Talk with your child about what they watch and post.
- Use Android's parental controls (Google Family Link) to manage app usage.
- Teach them to report and block anything that makes them uncomfortable.
- Remind them never to share personal information or meet strangers from the internet.

## How to report a problem

Tap the **options menu (⋮)** on a video and choose **Report Content**. For other concerns, use the [Contact page](/contact). Full guide: [how to report content on Bideo](/blog/how-to-report-content-on-bideo).
