A great video still needs to be found. Titles and hashtags tell viewers — and Bideo search — what your video is about. Here's how to write them so the right people discover your content.

## Why titles and hashtags matter

When someone searches Bideo for "aloo paratha recipe" or "funny school comedy", the app looks at titles, descriptions and tags. Clear words help your video appear for the searches that matter.

## How to write a great title

1. **Say what it is.** "5-minute aloo paratha recipe" beats "Must watch!!!"
2. **Put the main keyword first.** Start with the words people would search.
3. **Add a hook.** A number, a result or a question: "3 mistakes everyone makes…"
4. **Keep it short.** Most of the title should fit on screen.
5. **Use your audience's language.** Hindi, Hinglish, Bhojpuri, Tamil — whatever your viewers search in.

### Examples

| Weak title | Strong title |
|---|---|
| Wow 😍😍 | Street-style pani puri at home in 10 minutes |
| Part 2 | Village comedy part 2 — when papa checks the report card |
| New video | 3 easy study tips to remember answers longer |

## How to use hashtags

- Use **3 to 5 relevant hashtags**.
- Mix **broad** (#comedy, #recipe) and **specific** (#bhojpuricomedy, #mumbaistreetfood) tags.
- Create one **signature hashtag** for your series (#ChefPriyaDaily).
- Check that the hashtag actually matches your video.

## Write a short description

Add one or two lines describing the video and include your main keyword naturally. Mention if it's part of a series.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Clickbait** that doesn't match the video — viewers leave and don't come back.
- **Keyword stuffing** — repeating the same word many times.
- **Unrelated trending hashtags** — misleading tags can break the community guidelines.
- **ALL CAPS** titles — hard to read.

> **Tip:** Before uploading, search Bideo for your main keyword and see what titles similar videos use. Then make yours clearer.
