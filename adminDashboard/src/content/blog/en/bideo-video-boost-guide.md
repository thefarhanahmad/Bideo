A **Boost** gives one of your videos extra visibility in the Bideo feed for a set time. You pay with **coins**, not money. Here's how it works and how to get the most from it.

## Boost packages

| Duration | Coins | Saving |
|---|---|---|
| 1 hour | 100 | — |
| 3 hours | 250 | Save 50 coins |
| 6 hours | 500 | Save 100 coins |
| 24 hours | 1,000 | Save 1,400 coins |

Don't have enough coins? Learn [how to earn coins on Bideo](/blog/how-to-earn-coins-on-bideo).

## How a Boost works

- Your boosted video gets **priority placement** in the feed for the duration you choose.
- If many boosts are active, yours may be **queued** and start automatically when a slot opens.
- You can see your Boost status (queued, active, completed) in the app.

## When to boost

Boosts work best on videos that are **already good**:

- A video getting **more likes and comments than usual**.
- A **timely** video — festivals, a trending topic, a new series.
- Your **best introduction video** for new viewers.

Avoid boosting videos with weak hooks or poor quality — extra visibility won't fix a video people skip.

## Get more from every Boost

1. **Boost at the right time** — when your audience is online. See the [best time to post](/blog/best-time-to-post-short-videos-in-india).
2. **Strong first 2 seconds** — make the most of the extra viewers.
3. **Reply to comments quickly** while the Boost is running.
4. **Post a story** at the same time to bring followers too.
5. **Pick the right length** — try 1 or 3 hours first, then use 24 hours for proven videos.

> **Note:** A Boost increases visibility; it doesn't guarantee views, followers or earnings.
