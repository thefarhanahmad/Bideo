Before you can earn on Bideo, your channel goes through a simple two-step process. Here is exactly what is required and how the review works.

## Eligibility basics

- You must be an **adult** — minors cannot access monetized features.
- Your content must be **original** (made by you, or you have permission to use it).
- You must follow the **community guidelines** and **copyright policy**.

## Step 1: 3 approved original videos

You need **3 original videos** that pass the **monetization review**. Each uploaded video can be reviewed in one of these ways:

- **Pass via Ads:** watch **4 short rewarded ads** in the app to complete that video's review.
- **Team review:** the Bideo review team checks and approves the video.

Your progress (for example *2 of 3 passed*) is shown in **Creator Earnings & Payouts**, with an **Upload Next Video** shortcut.

### What makes a video pass

- It's your own original work.
- It doesn't contain re-uploaded TV shows, movie clips or other creators' videos.
- It follows the community guidelines (no harmful, explicit or misleading content).

## Step 2: Verification and payout details

After Step 1, the payout section unlocks. Submit:

| Detail | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Full legal name (as per bank/Aadhaar) | Identity check |
| Registered phone number | Account confirmation |
| Aadhaar number | Identity verification |
| UPI ID or bank account (account number, IFSC, bank name, beneficiary name) | Where your payouts are sent |

Tap **Submit Verification Details**. The team reviews the application; once approved, your channel shows **Monetized**.

> **Safety:** Enter these details only inside the official Bideo app. Bideo will never ask for your password or OTP over phone, WhatsApp or social media.

## After approval

Views on eligible videos and shorts generate earnings, settled into your wallet daily. Learn how to [withdraw your money](/blog/how-to-withdraw-money-from-bideo).

## Staying eligible

Monetization can be removed for:
- Copyright violations or re-uploaded content
- Fake views, followers or engagement
- Repeated community guideline violations

Keep posting original, quality videos and your channel stays in good standing.
