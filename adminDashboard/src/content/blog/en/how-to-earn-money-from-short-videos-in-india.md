Short videos can become a real source of income — but it takes time, consistency and original content. Here are the honest, proven ways Indian creators earn, and how to get started.

> **No guarantees:** Any app or person promising fixed or guaranteed daily income from videos should be treated with caution.

## 1. Platform monetization

Many video platforms share earnings with eligible creators based on views. On **Bideo**, eligible adult creators can join the monetization program after **3 approved original videos** and **verification**, then earn from views on eligible videos and withdraw from **₹1,000** to UPI or bank.

Read: [how to earn money on Bideo](/blog/how-to-earn-money-on-bideo).

## 2. Brand collaborations

As your audience grows, local and national brands may pay you to feature products. Tips:
- Build a clear niche so brands know your audience.
- Keep a simple media kit: followers, average views, audience location.
- Always **disclose paid partnerships** clearly to your viewers.

## 3. Affiliate marketing

Share product links and earn a commission on purchases. Recommend only products you've used, and tell viewers when a link is an affiliate link.

## 4. Sell your own products or services

Your videos can promote:
- Home-made food, crafts or clothing
- Tuition, coaching or online classes
- Services like photography, editing or makeup

## 5. Grow an audience first

Every method works better with a loyal audience:
- [Start a channel the right way](/blog/how-to-start-a-video-channel-beginners)
- [Grow followers](/blog/how-to-grow-followers-on-bideo)
- [Make videos people share](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-bideo)

## What to avoid

- **Paying to "unlock" earnings** — real platforms don't charge you to get paid.
- **Sharing OTPs or passwords** — no genuine team needs them.
- **Fake views or followers** — they break platform rules and can get you banned.
- **Copyrighted content** — re-uploads can be removed and demonetized.

## A simple 30-day starter plan

1. **Week 1:** pick a niche, set up your channel, post 3 videos.
2. **Week 2:** post 3–5 more, study what worked.
3. **Week 3:** start a series; engage with every comment.
4. **Week 4:** complete the [monetization steps](/blog/bideo-monetization-requirements) and keep posting.
