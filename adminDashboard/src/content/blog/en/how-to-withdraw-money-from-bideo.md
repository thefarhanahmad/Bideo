Once your channel is monetized and your wallet balance grows, you can withdraw your earnings to your **UPI ID** or **bank account**. Here's how it works.

## Before you can withdraw

- Your channel must be **monetized** — see [monetization requirements](/blog/bideo-monetization-requirements).
- Your **verification and payout details** must be submitted and approved.
- Your **Available Wallet Balance** must be at least **₹1,000**.

## Pending vs available balance

| Balance | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Recent earnings that haven't been settled yet |
| **Available** | Settled earnings you can withdraw |

Pending earnings are **settled into your available balance daily** (after midnight IST).

## How to request a payout

1. Open **Creator Earnings & Payouts** in the app.
2. Check your **Available Wallet Balance** (minimum ₹1,000).
3. Tap **Request Payout**.
4. **Select Payout Destination** — your UPI ID or bank account.
5. Enter the **Withdrawal Amount (₹)**.
6. Tap **Submit Withdrawal Request**.

## Track your payout

Your request appears in **Payout History** with its status:

- **Pending** — received and being processed
- **Approved** — processed, with a transfer reference where available
- **Rejected** — not processed; check the note and your payout details

## Tips for smooth payouts

- Double-check your **UPI ID** or **account number and IFSC** before submitting.
- Make sure the **name** matches your bank/Aadhaar.
- Don't share your login details or OTPs with anyone claiming to "speed up" payouts — Bideo staff will never ask for them.

> **Remember:** Earnings depend on views of your monetized videos and current rates. They are not guaranteed.
