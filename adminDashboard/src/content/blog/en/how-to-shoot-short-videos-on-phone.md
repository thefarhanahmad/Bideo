You don't need a professional camera to make great short videos. Today's phones are powerful — what makes the difference is **light, sound, framing and editing**. Here's how to get the most out of your phone.

## 1. Shoot vertical (9:16)

Shorts on Bideo are **vertical** and up to **60 seconds**. Hold your phone upright and keep the main subject in the middle, leaving some space at the top and bottom for the title and buttons.

## 2. Use good light

Light is the cheapest quality upgrade:
- **Face a window** during the day — soft daylight looks great.
- Avoid having a bright window **behind** you; it makes your face dark.
- At night, use one **ring light or desk lamp** in front of you, slightly above eye level.

## 3. Get clear sound

Viewers forgive average video but not bad audio.
- Record in a **quiet room**; soft furnishings reduce echo.
- Keep the phone close when speaking.
- A basic **clip-on microphone** is a great first purchase.

## 4. Keep shots steady

- Use a **tripod or phone stand**, or rest the phone on books.
- Turn on your phone's **video stabilisation** if available.
- Move slowly and smoothly when you must move.

## 5. Use your camera settings well

- Clean the lens before recording — a smudge makes everything blurry.
- Record in **1080p**; Bideo supports videos up to 1080p.
- Tap to focus on your face, and lock exposure if the brightness keeps changing.

## 6. Frame like a pro

- Keep your eyes in the **upper third** of the frame.
- Use a clean, uncluttered background.
- Mix close-ups with wider shots to keep it interesting.

## 7. Edit for pace

- Cut silences and repeated takes.
- Add captions — many people watch without sound.
- Keep text on screen short and easy to read.
- Use only music and clips you have the right to use.

## 8. Upload with a strong title

Write a clear title and add relevant hashtags. See the [titles and hashtags guide](/blog/short-video-titles-and-hashtags-guide).

> **Checklist before you post:** vertical ✔ well-lit ✔ clear sound ✔ strong first 2 seconds ✔ captions ✔ original content ✔
