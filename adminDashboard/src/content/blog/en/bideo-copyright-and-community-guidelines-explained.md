Bideo's rules exist to keep the platform safe, fair and enjoyable. Here's what they mean in plain language, so you can create confidently. (The full, official rules are on the [Community Guidelines](/guidelines) and [Copyright Policy](/copyright) pages.)

## What you can post

- Your **own original** videos, shorts, stories and posts
- Content you have **permission** to use
- Commentary, reviews and reactions that add your own value
- Creative, educational, entertaining and cultural content in any language

## What's not allowed

- **Nudity and sexual content**, and any content that sexualises minors
- **Hate speech** against any religion, caste, gender, region or community
- **Harassment and bullying**
- **Dangerous acts** that could cause harm
- **Scams, fraud and misleading claims**
- **Spam and fake engagement** — fake views, followers or likes
- **Copyright violations** — re-uploaded TV shows, movie clips or other creators' videos

## Copyright made simple

1. **Film your own clips.** It's the safest way.
2. **Don't re-upload** other people's videos, TV shows or films.
3. **Use music you're allowed to use.**
4. **Commentary must add value** — simply re-posting a clip with a caption isn't enough.
5. **Repeat infringement** can lead to suspension or termination.

If someone copies **your** content, the copyright owner can send a takedown notice using the details on the [Contact page](/contact).

## What happens after a violation

Depending on the issue, Bideo may:
- Remove the content
- Restrict its visibility or disable features
- Issue a warning
- Remove monetization
- Suspend or terminate the account

## Tips to stay safe

- When in doubt, don't post it.
- Keep titles and thumbnails honest.
- Respect everyone in the comments.
- Read the [monetization requirements](/blog/bideo-monetization-requirements) if you plan to earn.

## Next steps

See [how to report content on Bideo](/blog/how-to-report-content-on-bideo) if you find something that breaks these rules.
